Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your SipSpotter account and all associated data at any time. This includes your profile, saved venues, posts, reviews, photos, and any subscription records tied to your account.

How to request deletion

Email us from the address associated with your SipSpotter account and include the subject line "Account Deletion Request." We'll verify your identity and process the deletion within 30 days.

Email sipspotterapp@gmail.com

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, username, and account credentials
  • Posts, reviews, comments, and photos you've submitted
  • Saved/favorited venues and trip plans
  • Palate data (flavor preferences, taste history, Yearly Wrapped data)
  • Device tokens used for push notifications

What may be retained

Some information may be retained for a limited period where required for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or subscription-billing purposes (for example, transaction records related to a purchase made through the App Store or Google Play). This data is not used for any other purpose and is deleted once no longer legally required.
